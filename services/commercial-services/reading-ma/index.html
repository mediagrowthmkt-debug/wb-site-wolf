Permits & code

Commercial permits and code in Reading

Commercial construction in Reading runs under a stricter code than residential and requires permits from the Reading Building Department. Depending on the use and any change of occupancy, your project may trigger ADA accessibility upgrades, commercial egress and exit-sign requirements, fire-rated assemblies and sprinklers, and energy-code compliance. Missing one of these is the classic reason a business misses its opening date.

Wolf Carpenters manages the commercial code side for you. We pull the permits, coordinate the required plan review, schedule the building, electrical, plumbing and fire inspections, and build to the Massachusetts commercial code so your final inspection and certificate of occupancy go through cleanly. You focus on opening; we handle the town.

Change of use, the hidden cost to plan for

When a space changes use, say a retail unit becoming a restaurant or a medical office, the code often demands upgrades the previous tenant never needed: more parking-accessible routes, added restrooms, grease interceptors or upgraded ventilation. We identify a change-of-use scope early so it is priced and scheduled, not a surprise that blows the budget at inspection.
