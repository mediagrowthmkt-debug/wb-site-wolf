Hiring tips

What to ask before you hire a general contractor

A good general contractor should welcome hard questions. Before you sign anything in Peabody, ask:

  • Do you use subcontractors or an in-house crew? This tells you who is actually accountable on your job site day to day.
  • Is the estimate itemized and in writing? A vague lump sum makes it impossible to compare bids or know what you're paying for.
  • Who pulls the permits? A contractor who wants you to pull the permit may be avoiding responsibility for the work.
  • What is the written schedule and payment plan? Milestones protect both sides and keep the project moving.
  • Can I see recent local projects and reviews? Real North Shore work and verifiable Google reviews are the best proof.

One thing you never want to say to a contractor is "just do whatever you think is best" with no written scope, that is how budgets balloon. With Wolf Carpenters, everything is agreed and documented up front, so there are no surprises.

Permits

Permits & inspections in Peabody, MA

Most general contractor work in Peabody, additions, structural changes, decks, electrical, and plumbing, requires a permit through the City of Peabody Inspectional Services Department. We handle that for you: preparing the application, submitting plans, and scheduling the framing, electrical, plumbing, and final inspections so each phase is signed off before we move on.

Getting permitting right matters for two reasons beyond code: it protects your home's resale value, and it keeps your project on a predictable schedule. A contractor who skips permits to "save time" can leave you with a stop-work order or un-permitted work that has to be redone. That is exactly the kind of shortcut our in-house model exists to avoid.
