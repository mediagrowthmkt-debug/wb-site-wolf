Layouts

Kitchen layouts that work for North Andover homes

The layout is the biggest decision in a kitchen remodel because it drives cost, timeline and how the room works every day. Common layouts we build:

  • Galley: two parallel runs, efficient for narrow kitchens in older North Andover homes.
  • L-shape: two connected walls, flexible and open to a dining area.
  • U-shape: three walls of storage and counter, great for serious cooks.
  • Island: adds prep space, seating and storage; the most requested upgrade.
  • Peninsula: island-style seating when floor space is tight.
  • Open-concept: removing a wall to join the kitchen and living space.
  • Eat-in: room for a table, common in family homes.

In older North Andover homes, opening a wall for an open-concept kitchen often means checking whether it is load-bearing, something our in-house crew evaluates before design.

Timeline

Your kitchen remodel timeline, week by week

Every project is different, but a typical full kitchen remodel in North Andover moves through these stages:

  • Weeks 1-2: design, final measurements and material selection.
  • Weeks 2-3: permits pulled with the Town of North Andover.
  • Weeks 3-4: demolition and rough-in (electrical, plumbing, HVAC).
  • Weeks 4-6: drywall, flooring and cabinet installation.
  • Weeks 6-8: countertops templated and installed, backsplash and tile.
  • Weeks 8-10: lighting, fixtures, finish carpentry, paint and the final walkthrough.

Cabinet and countertop lead times are the most common reason a schedule shifts, which is why we order early and keep you posted at each stage.

Cost

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in North Andover?

Kitchen remodel pricing on the North Shore depends on the size of the kitchen, whether the layout changes, and the level of cabinetry and countertops you choose. As a general guide for North Andover and Massachusetts:

  • Minor / cosmetic remodel: roughly $25,000 to $45,000, refacing or stock cabinets, new countertops, backsplash and fixtures with the layout unchanged.
  • Mid-range full remodel: roughly $45,000 to $80,000, new semi-custom cabinets, quartz countertops, lighting, flooring and some layout changes.
  • High-end / custom remodel: $80,000 and up, custom cabinetry, islands, wall removal, and premium materials and appliances.

These are typical ranges, not a quote. The only way to know your real number is a walkthrough, and we give every North Andover homeowner a clear written estimate for free.

What moves the price

  • Kitchen size & square footage
  • Layout & wall changes
  • Custom vs. stock cabinetry
  • Countertop material
  • Moving plumbing or gas lines
  • Condition of an older home

Permits & materials

Permits and how long a kitchen remodel takes

A kitchen remodel in North Andover almost always needs a building permit from the Town of North Andover, plus separate electrical and plumbing permits when wiring or pipes are involved. Massachusetts requires licensed professionals for that work, and inspections happen at set stages. Wolf Carpenters handles the permitting so you do not have to chase paperwork.

Most full kitchen remodels take about 6 to 12 weeks from demolition to final walkthrough, depending on the scope, cabinet lead times and inspection scheduling. Cosmetic updates can be faster; jobs that move walls or plumbing take longer. We give you a realistic schedule up front and keep you posted as the work moves.

Avoid these

Kitchen remodel mistakes to avoid

  • Skipping permits: unpermitted work can stall a future home sale and fail inspection.
  • Choosing the cheapest bid: a low number often means subcontractors, thin scope or change orders later.
  • Locking the layout too late: moving plumbing or walls after demo starts adds cost and time.
  • Under-planning storage and lighting: the two things homeowners regret most.
  • Ignoring the age of the home: older North Andover houses often need wiring, plumbing or leveling that a walkthrough should catch up front.

How we help you avoid them

  • Written scope up front
  • Layout locked before demo
  • Permits handled for you
  • Older-home issues flagged early
