About Wolf Carpenters

Our Story

Building Peabody, MA & North Shore, One Project at a Time

Wolf Carpenters was founded with a single mission: to deliver superior construction and renovation services that stand the test of time. What started as a small carpentry operation has grown into a full-service construction company trusted by hundreds of homeowners and businesses across Peabody, MA & North Shore.

Led by Julio and his dedicated team, we bring decades of hands-on experience to every job, from a kitchen remodel to a complete commercial build-out. We're not just contractors; we're craftsmen who take pride in every nail we drive and every surface we finish.

Based in Peabody, MA, we serve all communities in the greater Boston area, including Salem, Lynn, Danvers, Beverly, and surrounding towns.

Our Values

What Drives Us

Every project we take on is guided by our core values, built into everything we do.

Integrity

We're honest, transparent, and direct. You'll always know what we're doing, why, and how much it costs. No surprises.

Quality

We use premium materials and time-tested techniques. Our work is built to last generations, because that's what craftsmanship means to us.

Community

We're part of the Peabody, MA & North Shore community. We hire locally, give back locally, and take pride in making our neighborhoods better.

Innovation

We stay current with the latest building techniques, materials, and design trends to bring you the best modern solutions.

Reliability

When we say we'll be there at 7am, we're there. We respect your time and honor every commitment we make.

Family First

We treat every client's home as if it were our own. Your family's safety, comfort, and satisfaction always come first.
